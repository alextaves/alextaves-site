The room turns toward you

A photographer, a browser, and the question of what a page is allowed to remember about the person reading it.

Every issue is built as a distinct room. You arrive and the light is already on, and something in the arrangement suggests you were expected. The scroll is not navigation. It is the descent itself, and the further down you go the colder the water gets. Nobody tells you this. You work it out somewhere around the second paragraph, when the sound changes.
